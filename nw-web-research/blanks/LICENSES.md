### Photo libraries to pick from by hand (licences allow commercial use)
- Pexels (https://www.pexels.com/license/): free commercial use, no attribution needed. You can't sell unaltered copies or imply endorsement. Its search API needs a key, so I didn't scrape it. Try searching "blank t-shirt flat lay", "t-shirt hanger white wall" or "folded black t-shirt".
- Unsplash (https://unsplash.com/license): free commercial use. You can't sell unaltered copies or build a competing image service. Its API also needs a key.
- Rawpixel public domain (https://www.rawpixel.com/category/53/public-domain): CC0, with more tee shots than the ones above.
- Wikimedia Commons: check each file. Many tee photos there are CC BY-SA, which allows commercial use but requires share-alike, so avoid those for brand assets.
- Mixkit: video and stock under the Mixkit licence. It has no blank-tee stills.
- Smithsonian Open Access: CC0, but its collection has only historical shirts, no modern blank tees.

All of these are reference or base material. Final product imagery should be Notion World's own garments, with no people, as the brief says.
