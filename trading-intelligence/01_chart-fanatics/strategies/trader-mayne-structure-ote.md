# Trader Mayne — Structure + OTE

**Swing-trading framework for forex, futures, and crypto.** Trend-following
pullback entries using **order blocks, breakers, and OTE**.

## Workflow

1. **HTF (daily/weekly):** identify a **clean market structure break**.
2. Mark the **POI that caused the break**: an order block or breaker block
   ([[concept_order_blocks_breakers]]).
3. Wait for price to **pull back into the POI** (ideally inside the OTE band,
   [[concept_premium_discount_ote]]).
4. **LTF confirmation:** a **breaker setup**, i.e., a failed move that
   **sweeps liquidity and reclaims structure**.
5. **Stop** beyond the liquidity sweep; target in the HTF trend direction.
