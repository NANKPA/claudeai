# Candace Lau — NQ Liquidity Sweep & Reversal

## Rules (T3, structure confirmed by T2 listing)

1. **HTF context** and **premium/discount** location ([[concept_premium_discount_ote]]).
2. Identify a **clear liquidity pool**: swing highs/lows, equal highs/lows,
   session extremes ([[concept_liquidity]]).
3. Wait for the **sweep**. *"The goal is not to enter simply because price
   reaches one of these areas; the important part is what happens when that
   liquidity is taken."*
4. **Confirmation:** an **IFVG** plus **strong displacement with speed**
   ([[concept_fair_value_gap]], [[concept_market_structure]]).
5. **Execute on the lower timeframe;** target the **next logical liquidity
   area** at a minimum **1:2 R:R** ([[concept_risk_reward_r_multiples]]).
