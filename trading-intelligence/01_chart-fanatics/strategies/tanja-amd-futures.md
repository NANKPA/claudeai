# Tanja Trades — AMD Futures Model

**Structured AMD for futures day trading** that **filters out chop by only
trading after liquidity sweeps and confirmed displacement.** Designed for
**clean moves during major news events**, with clear risk rules and strict
discipline (T3).

## Sequence

1. **Accumulation:** identify the range.
2. **Manipulation:** wait for the **liquidity sweep** out of the range
   ([[concept_liquidity]]).
3. **Distribution:** enter once the new direction is **confirmed by
   displacement / BOS** ([[concept_market_structure]]).

Lineage: Dow phases → Wyckoff spring/upthrust → ICT Power of Three
([[concept_amd_power_of_three]]).
