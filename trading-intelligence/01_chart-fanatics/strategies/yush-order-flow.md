# Trader Yush — Order Flow: Trapped Traders

**Futures scalping that uses order flow to find support/resistance zones.**
Trades are taken when price reaches a zone and shows confirmation via
**absorption, delta, and trapped traders** ([[concept_order_flow]]).

## Rules (T3)

- Flag **large orders: ~75+ lots on NQ, ~200+ on ES.**
- A valid zone needs **at least 2 confirmations** from the framework.
- **Entries are early, so size smaller and keep stops tight**
  ([[concept_position_sizing]]).
