# Umar Ashraf — 5-Stage Trader Framework

A **development** framework (L7), not an entry setup. Umar Ashraf is the
founder of Stock Market Lab.

## Foundational truths

- **Trading is hard.** If you expect ease, you'll quit early.
- **The goal is NOT (initially) to make money.**
- **You can't skip stages.**

## Stages

1. **Novice:** build a foundation through education, paper trading, and
   learning the markets. Choose markets and a style (day vs swing).
2. **Developing:** **data collection** and early strategy; avoid ego-driven
   and revenge trades ([[concept_trading_journal_review]]).
