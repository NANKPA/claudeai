# Marco Trades — Liquidity Grab Playbook

**Core:** trade **reversals after a stop-hunt sweep** of a key high or low.

## Rules (T2)

- **Buys:** wait for price to return and **trade below a low**, look for
  **confirmation**, buy *below the low*, and place the **stop below that low**.
- **Sells:** mirror at highs.
- Key highs and lows = liquidity pools ([[concept_liquidity]]).

Also live-trades the NY session on **Chart Fanatics Live**.
