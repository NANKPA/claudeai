# Marci Silfrain — Measured Move Trend Strategy

**Trader.** Futures trader billed as the **world's #2 futures trader**
(a trading-championship result; one video cites a 320% return). Her
Chart Fanatics episode is among the channel's top videos (~772K views per an
aggregator).

## Rules (T2 with T3 detail)

1. **Confirm the trend** (higher lows / lower highs) ([[concept_market_structure]]).
2. Wait for a **strong impulse followed by a pullback.**
3. **Draw a trendline across the pullback** (across pullback highs in a
   downtrend, lows in an uptrend) ([[concept_trendlines]]).
4. **Enter** on rejection at, or break of, the line in the trend direction.
5. **Target = measured move:** the next leg is projected to equal the prior
   impulse (Wyckoff "cause and effect" lineage).
6. **Context:** Bollinger Bands help spot continuation vs possible reversal
   ([[concept_bollinger_bands]]).
7. Third-party filter: favour the **first two trendline touches**; avoid
   later ones (T3).

## Philosophy (high-signal quotes, paraphrased)

- Spends hours daily **backtesting, sim trading, and paper trading**: more
  time on charts than live trading ([[concept_backtesting]]).
- **Plan to lose on every trade, and plan to lose the next 20 in a row**
  ([[concept_risk_of_ruin_drawdown]], [[concept_trading_psychology]]).
