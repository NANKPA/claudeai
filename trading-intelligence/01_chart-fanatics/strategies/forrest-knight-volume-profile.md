# Forrest Knight — Volume Profile Edge-to-Edge

1. **Location:** price reaches the **edge of a high-volume node** at a key
   auction level: **prior-day high/low, overnight high/low**
   ([[concept_volume_profile]]).
2. **Trigger:** a **signal candle** with a visible wick rejecting the area
   that **closes in the trade direction**. *Wait for the close; don't
   front-run it* ([[concept_trade_execution]]).
3. **Bias:** a higher-timeframe directional filter.
4. **Target:** **edge-to-edge**, from one side of value to the next through
   the clean path of a **low-volume area**.
